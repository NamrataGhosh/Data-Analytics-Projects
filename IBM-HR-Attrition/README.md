# IBM HR Attrition Analysis

##  Project Overview

The **IBM HR Attrition Analysis** project focuses on analyzing employee data to understand the factors that contribute to employee attrition.

The project involves data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and dashboard development to identify patterns related to employee demographics, job roles, income, satisfaction, and other factors that may influence employee turnover.

---

##  Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze employee attrition patterns.
* Identify factors associated with employee turnover.
* Understand attrition across different departments and job roles.
* Analyze the relationship between attrition and employee demographics, income, job satisfaction, and work experience.
* Create an interactive dashboard to present important findings and insights.

---

##  Dataset

The project uses the **IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset**.

The dataset contains employee-related information such as:

* Age
* Gender
* Department
* Job Role
* Monthly Income
* Job Satisfaction
* Years at Company
* Overtime
* Work-Life Balance
* Education
* Marital Status
* Job Level
* Attrition
* And other employee-related attributes

The dataset was used for understanding employee characteristics and analyzing factors associated with attrition.

##  Key Insights

The analysis helps identify several patterns related to employee attrition, such as:

* Employees working overtime show a higher tendency toward attrition.
* Attrition varies across different job roles and departments.
* Employees with lower job satisfaction can show higher attrition levels.
* Income and experience can have a relationship with employee retention.
* Younger and less experienced employees can show different attrition patterns compared with more experienced employees.
* Employee work-life balance and job-related factors can influence retention.

> **Note:** The dashboard should be referred to for the exact values and percentages of the analyzed metrics.
